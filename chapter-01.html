Learn this chapter step by step

Start with Topic 1 even if you are weak in the chapter. Each lesson first explains the basic idea before moving to visual reasoning and exam-level application.

1Chemical ChangeA chemical reaction changes one or more substances into new substances with different properties.›2Writing Chemical EquationsMove from word equations to symbolic chemical equations and learn what reactants, products and state symbols mean.›3Balancing Chemical EquationsUse the law of conservation of mass to balance atoms without changing chemical formulae.›4Combination ReactionsUnderstand reactions in which two or more substances combine to form a single product.›5Decomposition ReactionsSee how heat, electricity or light can split one compound into simpler substances.›6Displacement ReactionsLearn how a more reactive element can displace a less reactive element from its compound.›7Double Displacement and PrecipitationVisualise exchange of ions between compounds and formation of an insoluble precipitate.›8Oxidation and ReductionTrack gain and loss of oxygen and understand why oxidation and reduction occur together.›9Corrosion and RancidityConnect oxidation with rusting of metals and spoilage of fats and oils.›
