Learn this chapter step by step

Start with Topic 1 even if you are weak in the chapter. Each lesson first explains the basic idea before moving to visual reasoning and exam-level application.

1Physical Properties of MetalsExplore lustre, malleability, ductility, conductivity and sonority.›2Physical Properties of Non-metalsCompare common non-metal properties with those of metals and note important exceptions.›3Reaction with Oxygen and WaterCompare how different metals react with oxygen and water.›4Reaction with AcidsUnderstand how many metals react with dilute acids to release hydrogen gas.›5Reactivity SeriesArrange metals by relative reactivity and predict displacement reactions.›6Ionic CompoundsVisualise transfer of electrons and electrostatic attraction between oppositely charged ions.›7Occurrence and Extraction of MetalsConnect reactivity with how metals are obtained from ores.›8Refining of MetalsUnderstand the purpose of purification and the basic idea of electrolytic refining.›9Corrosion and PreventionStudy corrosion and methods such as painting, oiling, galvanising and alloying.›
