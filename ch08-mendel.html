Mendel's Experiments

Gregor Mendel studied inheritance in pea plants by crossing contrasting traits and analysing patterns in successive generations.

1. Learn from zero

Gregor Mendel studied inheritance in pea plants by crossing contrasting traits and analysing patterns in successive generations.

What you must understand

  • Mendel selected clear contrasting traits in pea plants.
  • In a monohybrid cross, one form of a trait can be dominant over another.
  • The recessive trait can reappear in the F2 generation.
  • Segregation explains separation of allele pairs during gamete formation.
  • A Punnett square predicts expected genotype combinations, not guaranteed outcomes for a small family.
