Human Eye

The human eye is an optical organ that refracts incoming light and forms a real, inverted image on the retina; the brain interprets the signals sent through the optic nerve.

1. Learn from zero

The human eye is an optical organ that refracts incoming light and forms a real, inverted image on the retina; the brain interprets the signals sent through the optic nerve.

What you must understand

  • Cornea provides much of the eye's refraction.
  • Iris controls pupil size and therefore light entry.
  • Eye lens fine-tunes focusing.
  • Ciliary muscles change lens curvature for accommodation.
  • Retina contains light-sensitive cells and converts light into nerve signals.
