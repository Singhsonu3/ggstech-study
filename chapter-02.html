Learn this chapter step by step

Start with Topic 1 even if you are weak in the chapter. Each lesson first explains the basic idea before moving to visual reasoning and exam-level application.

1Acids, Bases and IndicatorsUse litmus, turmeric and olfactory indicators to recognise acidic and basic substances.›2Acids and Bases in WaterUnderstand how acids produce hydrogen ions and bases produce hydroxide ions in aqueous solution.›3Reactions of Acids and BasesStudy reactions with metals, metal carbonates and metal oxides.›4NeutralisationSee how acids and bases react to form salt and water.›5The pH ScaleLearn the 0–14 pH scale and connect hydrogen ion concentration with acidity.›6Importance of pH in Everyday LifeExplore pH in digestion, tooth decay, soil and self-defence by organisms.›7Salts and Their FamiliesUnderstand how salts form and how their nature depends on the parent acid and base.›8Common Salt and Useful ChemicalsLearn about sodium hydroxide, bleaching powder, baking soda and washing soda.›9Water of Crystallisation and Plaster of ParisSee how water can be chemically associated with crystals and how gypsum forms Plaster of Paris.›
