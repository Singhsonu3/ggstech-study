Ohm's Law

At constant temperature, the current through an ohmic conductor is directly proportional to the potential difference across it: V = IR.

1. Learn from zero

At constant temperature, the current through an ohmic conductor is directly proportional to the potential difference across it: V = IR.

What you must understand

  • V is potential difference in volts (V).
  • I is current in amperes (A).
  • R is resistance in ohms (Ω).
  • For constant R, doubling V doubles I.
  • An ohmic conductor gives a straight-line V–I graph through the origin.
