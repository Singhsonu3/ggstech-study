Reproductive Health

Reproductive health includes physical and social well-being related to reproduction, informed choices, prevention of sexually transmitted infections, and methods to avoid unintended pregnancy.

1. Learn from zero

Reproductive health includes physical and social well-being related to reproduction, informed choices, prevention of sexually transmitted infections, and methods to avoid unintended pregnancy.

What you must understand

  • Contraception reduces the chance of unintended pregnancy.
  • Barrier methods such as condoms also reduce transmission risk for many STIs.
  • Oral contraceptive pills mainly act through hormonal control of ovulation.
  • Copper-T is an intrauterine contraceptive device.
  • Responsible reproductive health includes accurate information, hygiene and timely medical care.
